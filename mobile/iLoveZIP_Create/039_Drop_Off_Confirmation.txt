======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Ensure trips are completed only after reaching the destination while
accurately calculating all applicable charges.

Business Goals

• Prevent fraudulent trip completion

• Improve billing accuracy

• Better customer experience

• Proper trip closure
