2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Protect drivers by enabling instant communication with the MBconnect
Operations Control Room and emergency contacts.

Business Goals

• Driver Safety

• Fast Emergency Response

• Real-time Monitoring

• Incident Documentation

24. DEVELOPER NOTES
======================================================================

SOS button should remain visible on all active trip screens.

Silent SOS must not produce visual or audio confirmation beyond
what is necessary for safety.

Continue sending GPS updates in the background.

Retry automatically if connectivity is temporarily lost.

Support multiple evidence uploads.

Support localization.

Support dark mode.
