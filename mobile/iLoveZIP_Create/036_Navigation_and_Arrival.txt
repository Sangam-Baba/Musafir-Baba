======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Ensure the driver reaches the pickup location efficiently while keeping
the customer informed.

Business Goals

• Reduce pickup delays

• Improve ETA accuracy

• Minimize missed pickups

• Improve customer experience

25. DEVELOPER NOTES
======================================================================

Map rendering must remain smooth while receiving location updates.

Support automatic arrival detection using configurable geofencing.

Continue navigation in the background when the app is minimized.

Waiting charges should be configurable by city and trip type.

SOS button must remain visible throughout navigation.

Support localization.

Support dark mode.
