1. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
======================================================================

MBconnect follows an Enterprise Mobile Design Language.

Goals

• Simple
• Fast
• Clean
• Professional
• High readability
• Easy navigation
• Driver friendly
• Large touch targets

The interface should always prioritize usability over decoration.
