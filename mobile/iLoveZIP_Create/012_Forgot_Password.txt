======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

The Account Recovery module allows a registered driver to regain access
to the application if they are unable to authenticate using their
existing credentials.

For Version 1, this screen primarily supports OTP-based account
verification.

Future versions may also support password or PIN reset.
