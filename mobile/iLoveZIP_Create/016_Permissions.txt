2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

The Permissions screen educates drivers about why each permission is
required before the operating system displays permission dialogs.

Business Goals

• Improve permission acceptance rate

• Reduce confusion

• Improve document upload success

• Enable live tracking

• Enable customer communication
