2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Vehicle Details create the master profile for every vehicle operating
on MBconnect.

Business Goals

• Register commercial vehicles

• Enable trip allocation

• Calculate pricing

• Maintain fleet records

• Improve passenger confidence
