Purpose

withdrawals and settlements.

======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Bank verification ensures payouts are transferred only to the verified
bank account of the registered Driver Partner.

Business Goals

• Secure payouts

• Prevent fraud

• Reduce payment failures

• Improve settlement accuracy

• Enable instant payouts
