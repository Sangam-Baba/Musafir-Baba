Purpose

======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Selfie verification helps ensure that the registered driver matches
the submitted identity documents.

Business Goals

• Prevent impersonation

• Improve trust

• Reduce fraud

• Support manual verification

• Prepare for AI face verification
