Entry Point

Purpose

Verify the driver's Driving Licence to ensure legal eligibility to
operate commercial vehicles on the MBconnect platform.

======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Driving Licence verification ensures that only eligible and licensed
drivers are allowed to receive bookings.

Business Goals

• Verify licence authenticity

• Validate licence expiry

• Confirm transport eligibility

• Prevent duplicate registrations

• Improve customer safety
