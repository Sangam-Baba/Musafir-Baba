======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

The Welcome Onboarding screen prepares newly registered drivers for
verification by explaining the process and setting expectations.

Business Goals

• Increase onboarding completion
• Reduce confusion
• Explain benefits
• Improve document quality
• Reduce support requests

9. VERIFICATION JOURNEY
======================================================================

STEP 1

Identity Verification

Documents

• Aadhaar
• PAN
• Driving Licence

------------------------------------

STEP 2

Vehicle Verification

Documents

• RC
• Insurance
• PUC
• Fitness Certificate

------------------------------------

STEP 3

Bank Account

Information

• Account Number
• IFSC
• Cancelled Cheque (Optional)

------------------------------------

STEP 4

Background Check

Verification

• Criminal Record
• Driver History
• Profile Review

Go Online
