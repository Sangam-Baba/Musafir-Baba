1. MODULE OVERVIEW
======================================================================

Module Name

PAN Verification

Screen Code

VERIFY-003

Entry Point

Identity Verification Dashboard

Purpose

Verify the driver's Permanent Account Number (PAN) and compare the
details with the verified identity information.

======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

PAN verification is required for

• Identity validation

• Financial compliance

• Driver payouts

• Tax reporting

• Fraud prevention

Business Goals

• Verify PAN authenticity

• Reduce duplicate registrations

• Match identity records

• Enable financial settlements

9. PAN VALIDATION RULES
======================================================================

Format

AAAAA9999A

Length

10 Characters

Characters

Uppercase Only

Auto Convert

Yes

Reject

Lowercase

Special Characters

Invalid Pattern

Examples

ABCDE1234F ✓

abcde1234f ✓ (Auto Converted)

ABC12345F ✕
