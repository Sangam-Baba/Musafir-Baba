======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Aadhaar verification establishes the driver's identity before they
become an active Driver Partner.

Business Goals

• Verify genuine identity

• Reduce fraud

• Improve onboarding quality

• Enable regulatory compliance
