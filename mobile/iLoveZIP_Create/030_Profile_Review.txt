Purpose

======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

The Profile Review screen provides transparency into the verification
process and reduces incomplete onboarding.

Business Objectives

• Review submitted information

• Display verification progress

• Identify missing items

• Reduce approval delays

• Improve onboarding completion
