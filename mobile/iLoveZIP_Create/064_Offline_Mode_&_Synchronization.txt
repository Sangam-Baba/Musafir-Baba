Screen Code

Entry Point

Purpose

Allow MBconnect to continue operating with cached data during
network interruptions and synchronize automatically when
connectivity returns.

2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Maintain uninterrupted driver operations regardless of
network availability.

Business Goals

• Better reliability

• Reduced trip failures

• Zero data loss

• Automatic synchronization
