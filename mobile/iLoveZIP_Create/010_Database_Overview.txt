1. INTRODUCTION
======================================================================

MBconnect uses a relational database architecture designed for
enterprise-scale fleet management, booking operations, finance,
corporate travel and analytics.

The database must support

• Millions of bookings

• Thousands of drivers

• Multiple cities

• Corporate clients

• Fleet owners

• Real-time analytics
