1. INTRODUCTION
======================================================================

MBconnect is an enterprise-grade mobility management platform developed
by MusafirBaba Travels Pvt. Ltd.

The platform is designed to manage:

• Driver Partners
• Customers
• Fleet Owners
• Corporate Clients
• Travel Operations
• Finance
• Dispatch
• Fleet Analytics
• Executive Reporting

Unlike a traditional taxi application, MBconnect combines fleet
management, trip execution, finance, dispatch, analytics, and
business intelligence into a unified ecosystem.

The system is modular, scalable, API-first, and cloud-ready.

4. PLATFORM OVERVIEW
======================================================================

The complete ecosystem consists of four major platforms.

1. MBconnect Driver App

Purpose

Used by drivers to:

• Register
• Upload documents
• Receive bookings
• Navigate trips
• Track earnings
• Request support

------------------------------------------------------------

2. MBGO Customer App

Purpose

Allows customers to:

• Book rides
• Track drivers
• Make payments
• Share trips
• Contact support

------------------------------------------------------------

3. Admin & Operations Portal

Purpose

Used by operations teams to:

• Assign drivers
• Monitor trips
• Handle SOS events
• Manage support tickets
• Track fleet performance

------------------------------------------------------------

4. Executive Dashboard

Purpose

Provides business owners with:

• Revenue insights
• Fleet utilization
• Driver performance
• Booking trends
• AI recommendations

======================================================================

MBconnect is designed as a complete enterprise mobility ecosystem that
integrates driver management, customer bookings, fleet operations,
finance, analytics, and executive reporting into a unified platform.

The modular architecture ensures scalability from a single-city
deployment to nationwide operations while maintaining a consistent user
experience across all products.
