2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

The Background Check module enables MBconnect to review relevant
information before approving a Driver Partner.

Business Goals

• Passenger safety

• Platform trust

• Regulatory compliance

• Fraud prevention

• Risk assessment
