2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Pickup verification ensures the correct passenger boards the assigned
vehicle and prevents fraud or mistaken pickups.

Business Goals

• Passenger Safety

• Driver Safety

• Fraud Prevention

• Better Customer Experience

24. DEVELOPER NOTES
======================================================================

OTP expires after configurable timeout.

Support offline retry if connectivity is temporarily lost.

Waiting charges should update automatically.

Corporate bookings may bypass OTP through dispatcher approval.

Record all verification events for audit purposes.

Support localization.

Support dark mode.
