======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

This module captures the minimum information required to create a Driver
Partner profile before document verification begins.

Business Objectives

• Fast registration

• Reduce onboarding time

• Collect verified driver information

• Prevent duplicate registrations

• Improve conversion rate
