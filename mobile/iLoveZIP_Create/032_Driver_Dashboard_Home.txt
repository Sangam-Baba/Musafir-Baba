2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

The dashboard enables drivers to

• Go Online

• Receive Trips

• Track Earnings

• View Important Alerts

• Access Essential Features

Business Goals

• Reduce driver distraction

• Increase trip acceptance

• Improve earnings

• Faster navigation
