2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Provide a localized experience for drivers by supporting regional
languages and future international expansion.

Business Goals

• Improve accessibility

• Increase regional adoption

• Reduce language barriers

• Improve onboarding

22. DEVELOPER NOTES
======================================================================

All UI text should come from localization resource files.

Do not hardcode strings.

Support runtime language switching without logout.

Language packs should be versioned.

Cache downloaded resources.

Support localization for date, currency and number formats.
