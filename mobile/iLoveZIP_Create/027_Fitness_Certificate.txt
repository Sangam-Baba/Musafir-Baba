2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

The Fitness Certificate confirms that a commercial vehicle is
roadworthy and legally fit for passenger transportation.

Business Objectives

• Passenger Safety

• Government Compliance

• Fleet Quality

• Prevent Unsafe Vehicles
