======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Vehicle verification ensures every vehicle operating on MBconnect
meets safety, insurance and legal compliance requirements.

Business Goals

• Verify commercial vehicles

• Improve passenger safety

• Ensure regulatory compliance

• Reduce fraudulent vehicle registrations

• Maintain document validity
