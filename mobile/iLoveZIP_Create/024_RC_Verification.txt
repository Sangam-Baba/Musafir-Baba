Module Name

Screen Code

VEHICLE-003

Entry Point

Vehicle Details

Purpose

Verify the Registration Certificate (RC) and confirm vehicle ownership
and registration information.

======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

RC verification confirms that the submitted vehicle is legally
registered and eligible to operate on the MBconnect platform.

Business Goals

• Verify ownership

• Validate registration

• Confirm commercial usage

• Prevent duplicate registrations

• Improve passenger safety
