2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Enable Driver Partners to report issues and receive quick assistance
without leaving the trip workflow.

Business Goals

• Faster issue resolution

• Improve driver safety

• Reduce trip failures

• Increase driver confidence
