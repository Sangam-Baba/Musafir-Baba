======================================================================

Module Name

Trip Details

Screen Code

TRIP-002

Entry Point

Trip Accepted

Purpose

Display complete booking information before the driver starts
navigation to the pickup location.

======================================================================
2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Provide all operational information needed to execute the trip
efficiently.

Business Goals

• Reduce driver confusion

• Improve pickup experience

• Increase on-time arrivals

• Improve customer satisfaction

4. SCREEN OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Present every important detail of the assigned trip in one place,
allowing the driver to begin the journey confidently.
