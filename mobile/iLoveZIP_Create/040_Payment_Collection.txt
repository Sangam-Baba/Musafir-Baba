2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Ensure every completed trip is settled accurately and securely.

Business Goals

• Accurate settlements

• Multiple payment methods

• Faster reconciliation

• Reduced payment disputes

Tips should be stored separately from fare.
