2. BUSINESS PURPOSE
======================================================================

Insurance verification confirms that every vehicle on MBconnect is
covered by a valid insurance policy.

Business Goals

• Passenger safety

• Regulatory compliance

• Reduce legal risk

• Prevent uninsured operations

• Automated renewal reminders
