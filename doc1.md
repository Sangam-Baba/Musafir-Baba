# 4\. How MBGo Booking Data Flows

MBGo follows a managed booking model:

Customer → MBGo App → MusafirBaba Server → MusafirBaba Operations Team → Eligible MBConnect Partner → Trip

Bookings are not directly assigned by MBGo to MBConnect partners. The MusafirBaba operational team reviews/manages bookings and assigns them to an appropriate MBConnect partner. Necessary booking information may be shared with the assigned partner only to the extent reasonably required to fulfil the service.
